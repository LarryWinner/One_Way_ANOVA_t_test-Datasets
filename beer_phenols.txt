Dataset:  beer_phenols.csv

Source: V. Sanna and L. Pretti (2015). "Effect of Wine Barrel Aging or Sapa Addition on
Total Polyphenol Content and Antioxidant Activity of Some Italian Craft Beers," 
International Journal of Food Science Technology, Vol. 50, pp.700-707

Description: Total polyphenol content for 3 replicates from 8 brands of Italian craft beers.
Units = Gallic acid equivalents mg/Litre. Data generated from means and standard deviations
Brands and Alcohol %:
1 = Pilsner Urquell   4.5%
2 = Birra del Senatore   5.0%
3 = BB9   9.0%
4 = BB10   10.0%
5 = Nut    7.0%
6 = Red Poison   6.5%
7 = Terre        12.0%
8 = Lune         11.5%

Variable Names:
brand
polyphenols
