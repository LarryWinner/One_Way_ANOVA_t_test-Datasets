Dataset:  meniscus.dat

Source: P. Borden, J. Nyland, D.N.M. Caborn, D. Pienkowski (2003).
"Biomechanical Comparison of the FasT-Fix Meniscal Repair Suture System
with Vertical Mattress Sutures and Meniscus Arrows," The American Journal
of Sports Medicine, Vol. 31, #3, pp. 374-378.

Description: Comparison of 3 Fixation Methods wrt 3 responses, with 6
replicates (knees) per method.
Methods:
1=Vertical Suture
2=Meniscus Arrow
3=FasT-Fix

Responses:
Load at failure (N)
Displacement (mm)
Stiffness  (N/mm)

Variables/Columns
Method     8
Load at Failure 10-16
Displacement    18-24
Stiffness      26-32
