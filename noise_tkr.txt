Dataset: noise_tkr.csv

Source: M. Dagci and S.D. Oztekin (2025). "Noise Control
Methods and Music Therapy Effects on Patients with Total Knee
Replacement Surgery: A Randomized Controlled Trial," Noise & Health,
Vol. 27, pp. 853-862.

Description: Clinical trial to study effects of noise conditions on physical measures
during total knee replacement surgery. 25 subjects per treatment.
Treatments: 1=Active Noise Control (ANC), 2=Passive noise control (PNC), 3=Music, 4=Control
Physical measures: Pulse Rate, Systolic blood pressure, Diastolic blood pressure,
                  respiration rate, and body temperature (Should not treat responses as
                  being multivariate)
Data generated to match treatment means and standard deviations

Variable Names:
patientID
soundTrt     (Given above)
pulseRate
SBP   (mm Hg)
DBP   (mm Hg)
respRate
bodyTemp  (Celsius)
