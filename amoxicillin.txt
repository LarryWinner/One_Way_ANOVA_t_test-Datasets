Dataset: amoxicillin.csv

Source: M.B. Delgado Charro and J.L. Vila Jato (1992). "In Vivo Study of
Sustained-Release Formulations Containing Amoxicillin and Gelucire 64/02,"
International Journal of Pharmaceutics, Vol. 78, pp. 35-41.

Description: Study comparing 4 formulations in 16 subjects in terms of:
Total drug excretion in 12 hours (E12)
Mean Residence Time (MRT)
Variance Residence Time (VRT)
Formulations: 1=28% Gelucire 64/02 in formulaion, 2=23%, 3=18%, 4=Reference
Authors conducted Multivariate Analysis of Variance (MANOVA)

Variable Names:
subject
formulation
E12
MRT
VRT
