Dataset: charcoal_temp.csv

Source: S.P. Bergeron, R.L. Bradley, A. Munson, W. Parsons (2013).
"Physico-Chemical and Functional Characteristics of Soil Charcoal Produced
at Five Different Temperatures," Soil Biology & Biochemistry, Vol. 58,
pp. 140-146.

Description: Metabolic quotient of soils amended with charcoal produced at
5 temperatures (450C-850C by 100C) following 8 week incubation.
Data simulated to match treatment means and SDs.

Variables
Y = Metabolic Quotient
trt  (labelled 1-5)
temp (Degrees Celsius)
