Dataset:  bioequiv.sulf

Source: Y-M. Liu, K.E. Zhang, Y. Liu, et al (2012). "Pharmacokinetic Properties 
and Bioequivalence of Two Sulfadoxine/Pyrimethamine Fixed-Dose Combination
Tablets: A Parallel-Design Study in Healthy Chinese Male Volunteers,"
Clinical Therapeutics, Vol. 34, #11, pp. 2212-2220.

Description: Results of Bioequivalence test for 1-period
parallel groups trial with 23 in Test drug and 23 in Reference Drug (46 total subjects)
Data generated from means and standard deviations in Table I 
(Note that in the table, appears to be arithmetic means and sd's, not geometric, 
also see next to last paragraph on p.2216). Using these numbers, we get very similar results to Table II, p. 2219.

Step 1: Obtain 90% CI for mu_T - mu_R in terms of log(Y)
Step 2: Take 100*exp(meanDiff), 100*exp(LB) and 100*exp(UB)  
Step 3: See whether interval lies completely between (80, 125)

Variables:
measure   1=Cmax,  2=AUC_0-72
drug      1=Sulfadoxine, 2=Pyrimethamine
form      1=Test, 2=Reference
y         numeric value of reponse
