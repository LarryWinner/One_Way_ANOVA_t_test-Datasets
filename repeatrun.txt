Dataset: repeatrun.dat

Source: I. Rollo, C. Williams, A. Nevill (2008). "Repeatability of scores on a
Novel Test of Endurance Running Performance," Journal of Sports Sciences, Oct.
14, 1:8. [Epub ahead of print]

Description: Total distance covered in 1 hour (meters) on treadmill by 10 runners during 3
trials (separated by 1 week each).


Variables/Columns
Runner   7-8
Trial Number   16
Distance in meters  18-24
