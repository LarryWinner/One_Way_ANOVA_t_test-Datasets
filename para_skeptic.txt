Dataset: para_skeptic.dat

Source: L. Storm and M.A. Thalbourne (2005). "The Effect of Change in
Pro Attitude on Paranormal Performance: A Pilot Study Using Naive
and Sophisticated Skeptics," Journal of Scientific Exploration, Vol.19,
#1, pp.11-29.

Description: Experiment that 1) classified subjects as Believers or
skeptics with respect to psi (based on scores on ASGS test, and
2) Measured successful matches in 50 trials with 5 Zener card symbols
(star, waves, square, circle, and cross).  The expected count is 10 
under "no ESP" assumption


Variables/Coluns
Group  8  /* 1=Believer  (ASGS >=17), 2=Skeptic  (ASGS < 17)  */
Correct matches   15-16    /*  Out of 50  */
