Dataset:  apple_hair.dat

Source: A. Kamimura, T. Takahishi, and Y. Watanabe (2000). "Investigation
of Topical Application of Procyanidin B-2 from Apple to Identify its 
Potential Use as a Hair Growing Agent," Phytomedicine, Vol. 7, #6, pp. 529-536.

Description: Study with 10 control subjects and 19 treated subjects
for procyandin B-2 on hair growth. Total hairs (in 0.25 cm^2) at
0 months and 6 months. Terminal hairs (> 60 mum in diameter, in 0.25 cm^2)
at 0 months and 6 months.

Variables/Columns
Treatment  8   /* 1=Placebo,   2=PB-2  */
Total hairs @ 0 Months   12-16
Total hairs @ 6 Months   20-24
Total Hair Increase     26-32
Teminal Hairs @ 0 Months   34-40
Teminal Hairs @ 6 Months   42-48
Terminal Hair Increase   50-56
