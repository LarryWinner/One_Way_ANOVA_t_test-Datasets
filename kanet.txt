Dataset:   kanet.csv

Source: T.H. Holland (1902). "The Kanets of Kulu and Lahoul, Punjab: A
Study in Contact-Metamorhism," The Journal of the Anthropological
Institute of Great Britain and Ireland, Vol. 32, pp.96-123

Description: Anthropometic Measurements of 30 Lahoul Kanets and 60
Kulu Kanets in Panjub.

Variable names
name    
group (1=Lahoul, 2=Kulu)
age
height
armspan
seatHt     (seated height)
kneelHt    (kneeling height)
cubit
leftFtLen  (Left foot length)
