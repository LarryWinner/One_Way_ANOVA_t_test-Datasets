Dataset:  sealion_bark.csv

Source: H. AhonenA.J. Stow, R.G. Harcourt, I. Charrier (2014).
"Adult Male Australian Sea Lion Barking Calls Reveal Clear Geographical
Variations," Animal Behviour, Vol. 97, pp. 229-239.

Description: Duration (in milliseconds) of sea lion barking calls
at 7 locations in Australia. Each sea lion was recorded 20 times,
but no random effects were included.
Locations: 1=Lewis Island, 2=Liguanea Island, 3=Olive Island, 
4=Blefuscu Island, 5=Lilliput Island, 6=North Fisherman Island,
7=Beagle Island

Variables:
location
duration
