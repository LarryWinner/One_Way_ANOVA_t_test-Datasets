Dataset:  entozamoeba.dat

Source: A.M. Griffin and W.G. McCarten (1949). "Some Methods for the
Quantitative Study of Entozoic Amoebae in Cultures," The Journal of
Parasitology, Vol.35, #2,pp193-198


Description: Yields of entozoic amoebae in 5 treatment conditions.

Conditions:
1=None
2=Heat at 70C for 10min
3=Addition of 10% Formalin
4=Heat followed by formalin
5=Formalin followed, after 1 hr by heat

Variables/Columns
Treatment    8
Yield (x10^-4)   14-16
