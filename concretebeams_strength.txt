Dataset: concretebeams_strength.csv

Source: V. Colotti (2016). "Mechanical Shear Strength Model for 
Reinforced Concrete Beams Strengthened with FRP Materials," 
Construction and Building Materials." Vol. 124, pp. 855-865.

Description: Comparison of 2 methods of predicting shear strength
of reinforced concrete beams. Author considered the ratio of 
experimental to predicted values for n=200 beams tested.

Variables/Names
Beam ID   (Beam_ID)
Beam Specimen  (specimenn)
Beam Type  (BeamType)    S=side bonded, U=U wrapped, W=fully wrapped
Experimental Sheer Strength  (shr_stng)
Predicted based on author's model   (ss_mod1)
Predicted based on ACI    (ss_mod2)
