Marketing and Management, Vol. 13, #1, pp. 109-127.

in 4 Conditions:

Ad3: Female co-founder of porn mag for women, in jogging shorts/hoodie
Ad4: Female and male partially clothed couple in bed, faces cropped out
    of image. Female on top of male.
