Dataset: art_rmv_spots1.dat

Source: K.G. Eirk (1972). "An Experimental Evaluation of Accepted Methods
for Removing Spots and Stains from Works of Art on Paper," Bulletin of the
American Group. International Institute for Conservation of Historic and
and Artistic Works, Vol. 12, #2, pp. 82-87

Description: Results of Experiment to compare 34 methods of removing
stains from Aged paper. 10 reps/treatment. Data simulated to preserve
treatment means, SDs.

Variables/Columns
Trt     7-8
Brightness score   10-16
