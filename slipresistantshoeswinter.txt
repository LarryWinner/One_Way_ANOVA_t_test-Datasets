Dataset: slipresistantshoewinter.csv

Source: C. Aschan, M. Hirvonen, E. Rajamaki, T. Mannelin (2005). "Slip
Resistance of Oil Resistant and Non-Oil Resistant Footwear Outsoles in
Winter Conditions," Safety Science, Vol. 43, pp. 375-389

Description: Results of experiments of friction in icy conditions,
with 23 brands and 6 replicate measurements per brand. Data are mean and
SD for each set of measurements.
Brand characteristics: cleat length, oil resistance volume change,
oil restistand indicator, Hardness @20C, @-20C, Abrasion resistance, Density

Dynamic coefficient of friction scores: @0C, @-5C, @-20C, on steel surface
with glyeride

Variable Names:
Shoe
Cleat
ORVolD   /* oil resistance volume change % */
OilRes   /* 1=Yes, 0=No */
NumRep   /* always=6  */
Ice0Mn   /* DOCF at 0C Mean  */
Ice0SD   /*  "   "   " SD    */
IceM5Mn  /*  "   "  -5C Mean  */
IceM5SD  /*  "   "   "  SD   */
IceM20Mn /*  "   " -20C Mean  */
IceM20SD /*  "   "  "   SD    */
StlGlycMn /* Steel/Glyceride Mean */
StlGlycSD /*  "       "      SD  */
Hard20   /* Outsole Hardness @20C */
HardM20  /*   "       "      @-20C */
ChngPct  /* Percent Change */
AbrRes   /* Abrasion Resistance  */
Density
