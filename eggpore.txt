Dataset:  eggpore.dat

Source: C.Tyler and F.H. Geake (1953). "Studies on Egg Shells. III. - Some
Physical and Chemical characteristics of the Egg Shells of Domestic Hens,"
Journal of the Science of Food and Agriculture, Vol.4, Issue 12, pp. 587-596.

Description: Pore Count as number of pores per cm^2 for eggs among
14 hens. 1-Way Random Effects ANOVA.

Variables/Columns

Pore Count   12-16   /* Pores/cm^2  */
