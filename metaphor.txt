Dataset: metaphor.dat

Source: J.R. readence, R.S. baldwin, M.H. Head (1986). "Direct instruction
in Processing Metaphors," Journal of Reading Behavior, Vol. VXIII, #4,
pp. 325-339.

Description: Results of experiment comaring teaching metaphors by
direct explicit teaching (process instruction) and the current method
(basal instruction). Response is number of correct metaphors (out of 8) 
by the child.
